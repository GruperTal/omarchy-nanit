# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-23

First marketplace release.

- Live camera video inside the bar popup, with sound.
- Always-on audio that survives popup close, shell restart and reboot.
- Night light and white noise toggles, temperature and humidity in the header.
- One external mpv window, toggled from the header, middle-click or `o`.
- Nanit mark drawn natively in the theme color.
